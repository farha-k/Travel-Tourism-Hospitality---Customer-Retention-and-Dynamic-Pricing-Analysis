# Week 4: Insights and Business Recommendations

## Objective
To analyze dashboard results and generate business insights from hotel booking data for improving customer retention and pricing strategies.

---

# Key Insights Identified

## 1. City Hotels Receive More Bookings
- City Hotels recorded significantly higher bookings compared to Resort Hotels.
- This indicates higher customer demand in urban locations.

### Business Insight
Hotels in cities should focus more on customer engagement and operational efficiency due to high traffic.

---

## 2. High Cancellation Rate
- Around 44K bookings were canceled.
- Cancellation percentage is high compared to total bookings.

### Business Insight
Flexible booking policies and reminder notifications can help reduce cancellations.

---

## 3. Portugal Has Highest Customers
- Portugal (PRT) contributed the highest number of bookings.
- Most customers belong to European regions.

### Business Insight
Hotels can target Portugal customers using special offers and loyalty programs.

---

## 4. Customers Book Early
- Average lead time is around 104 days.
- Customers generally plan bookings far in advance.

### Business Insight
Dynamic pricing strategies can be implemented for early bookings.

---

## 5. City Hotels Have More Cancellations
- City Hotels experienced higher cancellation counts than Resort Hotels.

### Business Insight
City Hotels should improve cancellation management strategies and customer communication.

---

# Recommendations

- Introduce discount offers for non-cancelable bookings.
- Use customer retention programs for repeated guests.
- Apply seasonal pricing strategies based on demand.
- Send automated reminders before check-in dates.
- Improve customer experience to reduce cancellations.

---

# Outcome
The analysis helped identify customer behavior, booking trends, and cancellation patterns. These insights support better business decisions and improve revenue management strategies.

---

# Conclusion
Week 4 focused on extracting meaningful insights from the Power BI dashboard and providing business recommendations. The findings can help hotels improve pricing, reduce cancellations, and enhance customer satisfaction.
