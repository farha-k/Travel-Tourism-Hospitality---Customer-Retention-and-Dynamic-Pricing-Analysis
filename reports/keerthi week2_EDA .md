## Week 2: Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA)

### Objective
To analyze hotel booking data and identify patterns in bookings, cancellations, and customer behavior.

---

### Dataset
- Dataset: Hotel Booking Dataset  
- Records: ~119,390 rows  
- Columns: 32 features  
- Source: CSV file  

---

### Analysis Performed

#### 1. Hotel Type Analysis
- Compared City Hotel and Resort Hotel bookings  
- Used countplot visualization  

**Insight:**  
City Hotels have more bookings compared to Resort Hotels  

---

#### 2. Cancellation Analysis
- Analyzed booking cancellations  

**Insight:**  
High number of cancellations (~44k bookings canceled)  

---

#### 3. Country-wise Analysis
- Identified top 5 countries  

**Insight:**  
Most customers are from Portugal (PRT)  

---

#### 4. Lead Time Analysis
- Studied advance booking time  

**Insight:**  
Average lead time is around 100 days (customers book early)  

---

#### 5. Cancellation by Hotel Type
- Compared cancellations across hotel types  

**Insight:**  
City Hotels also show higher cancellations  

---

### Tools Used
- Python  
- Pandas  
- Seaborn  
- Matplotlib  

---

### Outcome
EDA helped understand:
- Booking trends  
- Cancellation behavior  
- Customer distribution  

---

### Conclusion
The analysis provides insights into hotel booking patterns and can help improve pricing strategies and customer retention.
